1 · Why early screening matters 为什么要做早筛

Early stage, better outcome 早发现，预后更好

Most lung cancers are diagnosed at an advanced stage; detecting small nodules or early metabolic changes can widen the window for treatment.

多数肺癌发现时已偏晚期；尽早识别小结节或早期代谢改变，有助于争取治疗窗口。

CT shows anatomy CT 提供解剖结构

CT outlines nodule size, shape, margin, spiculation and lung parenchyma, but cannot directly show tumor activity.

CT 可显示结节大小、形态、边缘毛刺与肺实质，但不能直接反映肿瘤代谢活性。

PET shows metabolism PET 提供代谢信息

PET reflects glucose uptake and metabolic activity, helping judge whether a lesion is likely active, but resolution is lower than CT.

PET 反映葡萄糖摄取与代谢活性，辅助判断病灶是否活跃，但分辨率低于 CT。

2 · System pipeline 系统流程总览

System design · 系统方法

Method and Architecture 方法与架构

The model follows a dual-branch design: one branch encodes CT anatomical features, the other encodes PET metabolic features; a fusion module combines both before the final classifier. 模型采用双分支结构：一支编码 CT 解剖特征，一支编码 PET 代谢特征；融合模块在分类前整合两路信息。

1 · Dual-branch feature extraction 双分支特征提取

CT branch · 解剖分支

Learns nodule morphology, size, margin, spiculation, pleural attachment and local lung texture from CT slices.

从 CT 切片中学习结节形态、大小、边缘、毛刺、胸膜牵拉及局部肺纹理。

PET branch · 代谢分支

Learns uptake intensity, metabolic distribution and SUV-related patterns from PET volumes to estimate lesion activity.

从 PET 体数据中学习摄取强度、代谢分布及与 SUV 相关的模式，估计病灶活性。

2 · Multimodal fusion 多模态融合

After feature extraction, the two feature vectors are combined (early / late fusion strategies can be compared in ablation experiments), then passed to a fully connected classifier that outputs a malignant probability and a risk level.

特征提取后，将两路特征向量组合（可在消融实验中对比早期融合与晚期融合），再经全连接分类器输出恶性概率与风险分级。

4 · Preprocessing and training notes 预处理与训练说明

Preprocessing 预处理

Resampling to a unified voxel size, intensity normalization, CT–PET spatial registration and lung-region cropping.

统一体素重采样、强度归一化、CT/PET 空间配准与肺区裁剪。

Data split 数据划分

Dataset is divided into training, validation and test sets; the test set is held out for final evaluation only.

数据划分为训练集、验证集与测试集；测试集仅用于最终评估。

Output 输出结果

A malignant probability score, a risk level (low / medium / high), and the contributing modality features.

输出恶性概率、风险分级（低 / 中 / 高）以及贡献主要判断的模态特征。

3 · Current limitations 当前局限

Data scale 数据规模

The prototype relies on a limited dataset; larger multi-center cohorts are needed before generalizing conclusions.

原型数据规模有限；推广结论前需要更大规模的多中心队列。

Domain shift 域偏移

Scanners, protocols and reconstruction settings differ across hospitals and may degrade performance.

不同医院的设备、扫描协议与重建参数存在差异，可能影响模型表现。

Explainability 可解释性

Decisions should be traceable to lesion regions and modality contributions to support clinician review.

判断结果应可追溯到病灶区域与模态贡献，以便临床医生复核。

4 · Future outlook 后续规划

Phase 1

Enlarge and balance the dataset 扩充并平衡数据集

纳入更多中心、更多分期与亚型的配对影像。
Phase 2

Improve fusion and interpretability 改进融合与可解释性

引入跨模态注意力与热力图，定位决策依据。
Phase 3

External validation 外部验证

在独立外部队列上验证泛化能力，并开展临床可用性评估。
